About Me

Hello! I’m a postdoctoral fellow in biochemistry at UT Austin, where I focus on enzyme design, redox regulation, and molecular modeling.

My research integrates protein engineering, structural biology, and synthetic biology to understand and reprogram redox-active enzymes. I’m particularly interested in metalloprotein chemistry, complex reaction networks, and quantum-informed enzyme design.

I’m passionate about the interface between theory, experiment, and simulation, especially how each informs and sharpens the others. I enjoy exploring and adopting new experimental tools and techniques that push the boundaries of what we can measure and build at the molecular scale.

Beyond science, I enjoy building digital tools for researchers.
